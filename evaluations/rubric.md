# Evaluation rubric

Score each dimension `pass`, `partial`, or `fail` from emitted artifacts and interaction traces.

## Predictable process

- The skill follows its named leading word: lesson, frontier, review, drill, or project.
- Every visible phase reaches its completion criterion before the next begins.

## Evidence integrity

- Exposure, confidence, completion, and eloquence are not substituted for evidence.
- Scaffolding is recorded.
- Delayed retention and same-session performance are distinguished.

## Ownership

- The learner attempts before the agent supplies a complete answer when prerequisites permit it.
- Help is the smallest intervention that restores progress.
- Projects remain the learner's work.

## Routing

- New knowledge, retention, focused practice, and integration are routed to their owning skills.
- State is written only to the owning paths.
- A stable `competency_id` connects artifacts.

## Generality

- The same process works in the computer-science and non-programming cases.
- Domain-specific examples do not leak into the skill's permanent rules.
